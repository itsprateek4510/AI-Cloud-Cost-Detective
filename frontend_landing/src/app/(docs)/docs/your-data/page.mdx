# Your data

<p className="lead">You should be able to leave with your data. This page explains what CloudLedger keeps, what you can export, and how deletion works.</p>

## What is stored

- The email address and name on your account.
- The cloud accounts and regions you connected.
- Cost and usage metadata read from those accounts.
- Findings, notes, and the reasons attached to them.

CloudLedger does not store your cloud credentials, because access uses a read-only role.

## Deleting your data

You can request deletion from **Settings** or by contacting us. [CONFIRM deletion steps and
cooling-off period.] Deleting a connection stops future scans; deleting your account removes
the workspace and its findings.

Before you delete anything, ask us for an export so you keep a copy.
