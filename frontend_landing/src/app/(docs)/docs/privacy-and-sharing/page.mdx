# Privacy and sharing

<p className="lead">CloudLedger reads cost and usage data, and nothing else. Access is read-only, limited to the accounts you choose, and removable at any time.</p>

## Read-only by default

CloudLedger never asks for write access. It cannot create, change, or delete a resource, and
it cannot move money. The connection is a role in your cloud account that you can audit and
revoke.

## What CloudLedger reads

- Cost and usage records for the accounts and regions you select.
- Resource metadata such as service name, region, tags, and identifiers.
- Your account email and workspace settings.

CloudLedger does not read application data, database contents, object contents, or customer
records. [CONFIRM this list against the final integration before publishing.]

## What it stores

Cost and usage metadata, findings, and the email address on your account. Cloud credentials
are not stored, because access is granted through a role rather than a key.

## Who can see your data

Today, access is limited to the people you invite to the workspace. Team roles and
per-account permissions are <span className="docs-badge">Coming soon</span>.

Sharing a finding with someone outside your workspace, or publishing a report link, is also
<span className="docs-badge">Coming soon</span>.

## Revoking access

Delete the role in your cloud account. CloudLedger loses access immediately, and no further
scans can run. Then remove the connection in settings.

## What CloudLedger never does

- It never changes or deletes a resource.
- It never invents a number.
- It never sells or shares your data.
- It never trains a model on your account data.
- It never uses ads or third-party trackers on this site.
