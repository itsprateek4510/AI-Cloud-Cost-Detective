# Review findings

<p className="lead">A finding is a specific cost problem with an estimated saving. The number is only useful if you can check it, so every finding shows its working.</p>

## Open a finding

From the dashboard, choose a finding to see:

- the resource and account it belongs to,
- the time window that was measured,
- the estimated monthly saving,
- and the cost and usage data behind the estimate.

If a number cannot be traced to that evidence, treat it as a bug and tell us.

## What the status means

- **Open** — nobody has reviewed it yet.
- **Expected** — someone marked it as intentional, with a reason. It stops appearing in the
  main list, and the reason stays on record.
- **Resolved** — the resource was fixed or removed. [CONFIRM how resolution is detected.]

## Fixing a finding

CloudLedger suggests a fix. It never applies one. For most AWS waste findings, the fix is a
change your team makes in the console, Terraform, or a ticket.

Fix pull requests and approvals are <span className="docs-badge">Coming soon</span>.

## Marking a finding as expected

If a resource is meant to be there, mark it as expected and write a short reason. This keeps
the list honest without deleting the history. You can undo it later.

## Savings are estimates

Savings are calculated from your usage and published pricing. Your discounts, credits, taxes,
and contract terms can change the real number. The estimate is a direction, not an invoice.
