# Weekly summary

<p className="lead">Once a week, CloudLedger sends a short summary: what you spent, what changed, and what is still recoverable. It should take about two minutes to read.</p>

## What the summary includes

- Total spend for the period, compared with the week before.
- The services that moved the most, up or down.
- New findings, ranked by estimated monthly saving.
- The findings nobody has reviewed yet.

## Where it arrives

Choose email, Slack, or both in **Settings → Notifications**. One message per workspace per
week. You can add more recipients when team roles arrive.

## When it arrives

The summary is sent on Monday morning in your workspace time zone. [CONFIRM send time and
whether it can be changed.]

## Turning it off

Switch the summary off in **Settings → Notifications**. Turning it off does not affect the
dashboard or the findings.

## If it does not arrive

Check the address or Slack connection in settings, then look in spam. If the summary still
does not arrive, contact us from the [contact page](/contact).
